# PEAK Review System — User Guide

A concise guide to navigating and utilizing the PEAK Review Center platform for both **Administrators** and **Students**.

---

## 1. Student Guide

### Getting Started & Navigation
- **Login**: Access the system using your designated Student ID (or email) and password.
- **Student Dashboard**: Provides an immediate overview of your enrolled review program, active subjects, upcoming mock exams, and overall progress.

### Modules & Learning Materials
1. **Subjects & Lessons**:
   - Access study modules and syllabus topics under your assigned board examination program.
   - Read embedded documents, view reference PDFs, and follow curriculum progression.
2. **Quizzes & Assignments**:
   - Take lesson-specific quizzes with automatic score evaluation.
   - Review your answers, rationales, and retry attempts according to quiz settings.

### Mock Exams & Assessments
1. **Taking an Examination**:
   - Open **Mock Exams** from your navigation menu.
   - When an exam is open, click **Start Exam** to begin. If timed, keep track of the countdown clock in the top floating bar.
   - Questions can be answered sequentially or reviewed before final submission.
2. **Submissions & Results**:
   - Once submitted, view your score summary, total points, and answer explanations (if enabled by administrators).

---

## 2. Administrator Guide

### Administrative Overview
The administrator control panel allows staff, coordinators, and directors to manage review programs, exams, users, and readiness metrics.

### Key Administrative Modules
1. **User Management (`/admin/users`)**:
   - **Directory**: View, search, filter, and edit Students, Teachers, and Administrators.
   - **Admissions & Pre-Registrations**: Approve or process student applications and batch enrollments.
   - **Subscriptions**: Track enrollment validity and review batch access windows.

2. **Curriculum Manager (`/admin/curriculum`)**:
   - **Subject & Lesson Builder**: Create learning materials with modular blocks, PDFs, and integrated lesson quizzes.
   - **RapidOCR Scanner**: Built into quiz settings to instantly scan and extract questionnaire images or PDFs into multiple-choice items.

3. **Mock Exams (`/admin/mock-exams`)**:
   - **Exam Builder**: Create and configure timed board examination simulators.
   - **Scheduling**: Set unlock dates, deadlines, time limits, and program/major target filters.
   - **RapidOCR Toolbox**: Fast-extract questions from reviewers directly into the exam repository.

4. **Master Data & Settings (`/admin/master-data`)**:
   - **Examinations, Programs & Majors**: Maintain the structural tree of board examinations (e.g., LEPT, PNLE, CPALE).
   - **Batch Management**: Configure review cohorts, calendar years, and active session windows.
   - **Gradebook**: Generate readiness matrices, input mock exam scores, calculate ratings, and export reports.
   - **Roles and Limitation**: Configure granular dashboard access permissions and custom coordinator privileges.
